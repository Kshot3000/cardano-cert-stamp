Stamp certificates

For trainers, schools and contractors. One wallet signature stamps a whole class.

How it works

  1. Your browser makes a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of each certificate, mixed with a secret random salt.
  2. All fingerprints in a batch are combined into one Merkle root. That single root goes on Cardano in one transaction (about 0.17 ADA in network fees for the whole batch).
  3. Each certificate gets a QR code carrying its details, salt and proof. Scanning it re-checks the fingerprint against the chain.

On-chain format: CIP-190 proof-of-existence record, metadata label 309. Names, grades and emails never go on-chain.
